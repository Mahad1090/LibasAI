Frequently Asked

Is LibasAI an online retailer?

No. LibasAI is an intelligent search, recommendation, and discovery assistant. When you wish to purchase an outfit, we direct you to the official Pakistani brand store.

How does custom stitching work?

You can pair any unstitched look with a verified master tailor, enter your measurements once, and receive bespoke tailored attire at your doorstep with our Fit Guarantee.
