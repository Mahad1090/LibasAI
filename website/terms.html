1. Discovery & Redirect Model

LibasAI functions strictly as an AI-powered conversational search engine, recommendation system, and brand discovery aggregator for Pakistani fashion. LibasAI does not operate warehouses, maintain retail inventory, process garment sales transactions, or handle direct merchant checkouts. All product purchases occur directly on the respective apparel brand's official ecommerce website.

2. Bespoke Tailoring Network

The LibasAI Tailor Module facilitates communication and booking between customers and independent verified master tailors. While LibasAI enforces quality verification standards, measurement recording tools, and the Fit Guarantee policy, stitching execution, cutting, and finishing are performed by independent master artisans.

3. Intellectual Property & Brand Attribution

All trademarks, logos, brand names, product photographs, and fabric designs displayed on LibasAI belong to their respective Pakistani apparel brands (including Sana Safinaz, Gul Ahmed, Zellbury, Beechtree, Vanya, Suffuse, and others). Their presentation on LibasAI is intended solely for aggregated discovery, fair comparison, and academic demonstration under Section 14 of the FAST-NUCES FYP guidelines.

4. AI Recommendations & Fair Visibility

Recommendations generated by LibasAI use algorithmic post-hoc re-ranking to balance user preference relevance with equitable exposure for small and emerging local labels. Outputs are generated by machine learning models and may vary based on query phrasing.
