Ask LibasAI
FAST-NUCES Final Year Project · 2026–2027

Empowering Pakistani Fashion Through Artificial Intelligence & Master Craft

LibasAI is an AI-powered conversational shopping assistant that aggregates 80+ Pakistani apparel brands, solves the relevance-fairness trade-off for emerging local designers, and bridges fabric discovery with master bespoke tailoring.

The Real-World Challenge

The Fragmented Pakistani E-Commerce Landscape

Online apparel shoppers in Pakistan spend excessive time jumping across dozens of separate brand websites to compare fabrics, cuts, sizes, and pricing. Conventional search relying solely on exact keywords and rigid filters fails to understand conversational intent like "Show me olive festive raw silk kurtas under PKR 5,000 for an outdoor dholak".

Simultaneously, small and emerging Pakistani fashion labels struggle to compete against retail titans with massive marketing budgets. Purely popularity-based recommendation algorithms naturally reinforce this bias, starving young design houses of visibility.

The LibasAI Solution

  • Cross-Brand Aggregation: Unified search index across 80+ Pakistani brands without requiring individual site hopping.
  • Multi-Agent AI Pipeline: Specialized collaborative AI agents for search, fair recommendation, multi-brand comparison, and outfit styling.
  • Computer Vision: CLIP-based visual image search to find matching apparel by snapping a picture or uploading an Instagram look.
  • Fair Visibility Re-Ranking: Algorithmic post-hoc re-ranking ensuring small and emerging artisans gain equitable shelf space.
  • Master Tailor Integration: Seamlessly bridge from discovering unstitched fabrics to having verified ustaad tailors cut and stitch your suit.
System Architecture

Collaborative Multi-Agent Intelligence

Four dedicated AI agents operating concurrently to deliver instantaneous, tailored fashion recommendations.

1. Search Agent

Parses natural language user queries, extracts semantic constraints (budget, fabric, occasion, color), and queries vector embeddings via pgvector and full-text indexes.

2. Recommendation Agent

Implements Matrix Factorization and post-hoc bounded re-ranking to balance personalized user relevance against equitable visibility for emerging local labels.

3. Comparison Agent

Evaluates candidate items side-by-side on fabric composition, price per piece, delivery timelines, and returns policy to generate objective trade-off verdicts.

4. Outfit Agent

Calculates complementary color harmonies and cultural dress codes, automatically pairing primary garments with matching dupattas, footwear, and Kundan jewellery.
