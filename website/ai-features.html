The Intelligence Behind LibasAI

Four AI Engines.
One Seamless Wardrobe.

LibasAI connects you directly to over 80 Pakistani fashion labels with zero checkout friction or markups. Explore our specialized AI tools designed to help you find the exact style, value, and look you want.

01

Ask LibasAI (Conversational)

Natural-language fashion search that understands nuances like "rust kurta for an autumn wedding under Rs. 6,000" without clumsy keyword filtering. It extracts fabric, occasion, and pricing parameters automatically.

Try asking: "Find a 3-piece unstitched lawn suit under Rs. 5k from Gul Ahmed or Zellbury"

02

Visual Image Search

Drop a screenshot from Instagram, Pinterest, or a family photo. Our computer vision engine identifies the silhouette, fabric texture, and embroidery pattern, searching through live catalog inventory across all brands.

Key capability: Matches specific garment regions like neckline embroidery, sleeve cutwork, and print colors.

Stop opening 15 browser tabs across different brand websites. Compare similar looks side-by-side with automated breakdowns of fabric quality, stitch type, shipping timelines, and our proprietary AI Value rating.

Complete the ensemble effortlessly. Pick any hero kurta or suit, and LibasAI coordinates complementary dupattas, pants, traditional khussas, and artisanal jewelry, with real-time color harmony scoring.

Multi-Agent Architecture

Four Specialized Agents Working in Concert

Rather than relying on generic LLM prompts, LibasAI deploys four specialized autonomous agents for each shopping task.

Search Agent

Parses intent, queries product indexes, and handles Pakistani fashion terminology (pret, lawn, zari, khussa).

Recommendation Agent

Ranks matches based on your individual budget, brand affinity, and past interaction history.

Comparison Agent

Extracts fabric specifications, calculates real discount percentages, and assesses delivery guarantees.

Outfit Agent

Applies fashion color wheel rules and occasion dress codes to suggest harmonious cross-brand pairings.
