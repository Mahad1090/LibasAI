Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 · FAST-NUCES Research Demonstration

1. Measurement Data Privacy

Bespoke body measurements provided by users (such as shoulder, chest, waist, and length dimensions) are stored locally in your browser session or encrypted personal profile. Measurements are transmitted strictly to the selected master tailor for the sole purpose of garment cutting and bespoke stitching.

2. Visual Image Search & Photo Uploads

Photos uploaded to the LibasAI Visual Search engine or stitching inspiration dropzones are analyzed by Computer Vision feature extractors (CLIP / embeddings) solely to identify colors, silhouettes, motifs, and patterns. Images are not shared with third-party advertisers or sold to commercial data brokers.

3. Cookies & Session Storage

LibasAI uses standard browser localStorage to maintain your saved outfits, wishlist items, and stitching requests without requiring intrusive tracking cookies or personal data profiling.

4. Academic & Research Context

LibasAI is developed as an academic Final Year Project at the Department of Computer Science, FAST-NUCES Islamabad. Interaction metrics collected during prototype testing are anonymized and utilized solely to evaluate multi-agent task completion and recommendation fairness.
